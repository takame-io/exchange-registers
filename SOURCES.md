Each register's own terms apply to its data in `registers/` and to the company names in the other files. Takame reformatted the data (column names, date format, joined fields); nothing else was changed.

- Terms: [ESMA legal notice: reproduction authorised provided the source is acknowledged](https://esma.europa.eu/legal-notice)
- Attribution: Source: European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA), interim MiCA register.

- Terms: [ESMA legal notice: reproduction authorised provided the source is acknowledged](https://esma.europa.eu/legal-notice)
